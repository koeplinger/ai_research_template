# Notes

*Created 3 September 2026; updated 3 September 2026.*

Dated working notes: `YYYY-MM-DD_short_name.md`. A note is a dated record
of what a sweep, an audit, a reading, a day in the archive, a cleaning or
run, or a line of reasoning found on that day. Its subject is an event,
so it is exempt from the narrative rule and frozen on writing, or with the
plan that owns it (`DOCUMENT_GENRES.md`, *Dated records*). Copy
[`_template.md`](_template.md) to start one.

## Two kinds

This folder distinguishes two kinds of note, and a note says which in its
first line.

- **A working note.** A sketch that speculates, or a record of the day
  without inference: a reading log, an archive day, a cleaning or run log.
  Never cited as a result; what it records enters the record through a
  plan and its checks.
- **An analytical note.** Construction, sanity checks, and second pass
  recorded (`CHECK_METHODOLOGY.md` §8), serving a claim, a check, or a
  plan. It is cited by path from a claim's *Evidence and route* as
  material of the route; it backs nothing. A derivation or reading that
  *establishes* a claim is written as a check, not as a note.

The decision rule: analytical if a claim, check, or correction round will
cite it; otherwise a working note. A working note may later be superseded
by an analytical one; when in doubt, working.

A pre-registration protocol is an analytical note written before the
evidence is consulted, its commit evidencing the date, and named as the
locator of a check's `Pre-registered` stamp. It carries no `Plan:` line,
so it is frozen on writing; the check that names it is the plan-owned
artifact.
