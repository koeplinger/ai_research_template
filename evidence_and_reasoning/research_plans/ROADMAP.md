## What may be relied on

What may be relied on, and at what authority, is `PRECEDENCE.md`, *The
order of authority*: a claim speaks from the tier its register earns,
open plan or closed. A claim of an open plan is current state and may
still change (`DOCUMENT_GENRES.md`, *Artifacts of an open plan*); an
imported result enters under `../../inherited/README.md`
(`MANIFESTO.md` §6); a sketch or a draft plan is never cited as a result.
