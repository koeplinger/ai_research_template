# A note on the market days of the town, 1851

*A fictional secondary source, written for this example. Page numbers are marked in brackets.*

[p. 1]

The town's market roll for the year 1851 records forty-eight market
days. The market was held on Tuesdays throughout the year, with the
customary interruptions at the turn of the year and in the depth of
winter, and the roll shows no day held on any other day of the week.

[p. 2]

The roll for 1852 is fuller, and we do not treat it here. A count of
the market days of 1852 is left to a later note.
