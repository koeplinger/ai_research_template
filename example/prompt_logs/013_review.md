The refuters refuted 5 of the 24, narrowed 18, and let 1 stand. What the
referee found that matters most:

- The write-up never answered its own question. It asked whether the roll
  supports the note, and no sentence said yes, no, or in part.
- It rested four statements of what the note does not say on a quotation
  without saying that the quotation is the whole of the note's first page,
  or what the second holds.
- It stated as a fact of the roll that a row is a day held, where that is
  the project's rule of reading, and it gave no ground for reading a date
  without a row as a day not held. The program that writes the roll gives
  that ground, and the page did not say so.
- Its title read as a study of real documents.
- Table 2 was captioned as read from the roll, and its weekdays are
  computed, on a calendar this project imposes.
- The fairs' names, the only ground for the northern seasons, appeared
  nowhere before the sentence that leaned on them.
- It did not name one open explanation of the difference: that the note
  read the roll in a state other than the one held.
