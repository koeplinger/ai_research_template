## The roll's count against the note's figure

The roll holds 51 market days in 1851, 49 ordinary and 2 fairs
[claim 001]. The note states forty-eight for 1851 [claim 002]. Repeated
rows do not explain the difference: no row of 1851 repeats an earlier
row's date and kind [claim 004].

## The roll's weekdays against what the note says of them

The roll's 49 ordinary rows of 1851 fall on Tuesdays and its 2 fairs on
Thursdays, and of the year's 52 Tuesdays three have no row, 4 February,
12 August and 30 December [claim 005]. Where what the note states of the same
days [claim 002] parts from these is the tension ledger's [tension T002]
[tension T003] [tension T004].
