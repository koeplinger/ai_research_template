## What is fixed

A row of the roll [Roll1851] is a market day of 1851 when its `date`
falls in the calendar year 1851, whatever its `kind`; fairs count as days
held. The count is the number of such rows. The monthly sums are the
counts per calendar month, and the second route is that they add to the
count. The decision rule: the count is reported as it comes out; no row
is excluded for any reason after the fact. Stopping rule: one run.
