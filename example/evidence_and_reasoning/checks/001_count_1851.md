## 1. What is checked

That the roll [Roll1851] holds 51 rows dated in 1851, of which 49 are
ordinary days and 2 are fairs, with monthly sums that add to the same
total. The rule of reading was fixed in a protocol note before this
check read the roll (`evidence_and_reasoning/notes/2026-09-06_counting_protocol.md`);
the count itself was set when the roll was generated (prompt 002), so
the protocol fixes the rule and not the outcome.

## 2. Construction

The program `python_project/src/check_001_count_1851.py` reads every row
of the roll into a list of (date, kind); a row belongs to 1851 when
its date's year is 1851, the calendar year being the frame. The count is
the length of that list; the monthly sums are the counts per calendar
month; the kinds are `ordinary` and `fair`, the roll's own values.

## 3. Sanity checks

The roll parses; every date is a valid calendar date; every kind is
one of the two the registry entry names; no row is dated outside 1851 or
1852.
