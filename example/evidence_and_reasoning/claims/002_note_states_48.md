## Claim

The note [Note1851, p. 1] states that the town's market roll for 1851
records forty-eight market days, held on Tuesdays throughout the year
with the customary interruptions at the turn of the year and in the
depth of winter, and that the roll shows no day held on any other day of
the week. This is what the note says, derived from its collation; the
claim makes no claim that what it says is true. The form consulted, the
note as held at its recorded checksum, read at its page marks, is
imposed: it is the instrument of record.

## What is not claimed

Nothing about whether forty-eight is right; nothing about the convention
under which the note's author counted; nothing about 1852, for which the
note states no count [Note1851, p. 2].
