## 0. How to read this, and what it sits on

At inception the thesis rests on nothing the project has established:
no claim exists yet. It rests on two files the project makes and holds,
the roll and the note. The reading order for a session arriving cold is
`evidence_and_reasoning/README.md`'s.

## 1. The hypotheses

### H-A. The roll and the note disagree on 1851

The roll's count of market days held in 1851 differs from the count the
note states for that year.

*What it takes as given.* The roll's dates, read as the roll gives them
(derived); the calendar year as the year's boundary (imposed: a choice of
this project, the roll being synthetic); a row of the roll, whatever its
kind, as a market day held (imposed: the rule of reading,
`evidence_and_reasoning/notes/2026-11-20_counting_protocol.md`); the
note's figure as collated against the note (derived from the collation,
never from memory).

*What would count against it.* A count of the roll's 1851 rows, by a
check that would fail were it otherwise, equal to the note's figure as
collated against the note.

The plans serving it are those whose `Serves:` line names it
(`research_plans/ROADMAP.md`).

## 2. The lines of work

- Count the roll's 1851 rows by a check that would fail were the count
  otherwise, and collate the note's stated figure against the note
  (serves H-A).
- Where the two figures differ, decide what the roll itself can decide
  about the difference, and set aside to a plan of its own what it
  cannot (serves H-A).

## 3. What is deliberately not pursued

- The count for 1852, 20 November 2026: the question is what the note
  states of 1851.
