# Plan 002: the note's counting convention

*Created 6 September 2026; updated 6 September 2026.*
Status: DRAFT
Serves: H-A
Prerequisites: 001

**Hypothesis plan.**

## The aspect, isolated

Why the note's figure for 1851 differs from the roll's count by
three: whether the note's author counted under a year that does not
begin on 1 January, or counted ordinary Tuesdays only, or neither. Not
the count itself, which plan 001 settled.

## Hypothesis

The note's figure results from a counting convention that differs from
the roll's: a year beginning on 25 March, or ordinary days only
([claim 005], [claim 006]).

## Design

The roll [Roll1851] recounted under each candidate convention by a
program; the note [Note1] re-read for any statement of its convention.
Each convention is an imposed choice of the recount, stated as such.

## Tasks

1. **Recount the roll under a year beginning on 25 March** and under
   ordinary days only, and compare each with the note's figure.
2. **Re-read the note** for any statement of its convention.

## Sanity checks

Every check of plan 001 still holds; the round check is clean.

## Deliverables

A claim per convention tested, with its check.

## Confirmation and refutation

CONFIRMED if one recount equals the note's figure and the note's text is
compatible with that convention; REFUTED if no candidate convention
reproduces the figure. An outcome that decides neither way closes
ABANDONED with that outcome as its reason.

## Out of scope

The count under the calendar year, settled by plan 001.
