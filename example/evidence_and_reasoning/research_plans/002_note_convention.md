# Plan 002: the note's counting convention

*Created 21 November 2026; updated 21 November 2026.*
Status: DRAFT
Serves: none, the note's counting convention is the question plan 001 sets aside, and the keystone states no hypothesis on it
Prerequisites: 001

**Plan with no hypothesis**, closing COMPLETE on the criterion under
*Confirmation and refutation*.

## The aspect, isolated

Which, if any, of a stated list of counting conventions, applied to the
roll, gives the note's forty-eight market days for 1851. Not why the
note's author counted as they did, which neither file can say; not any
convention off the list; and not the count for 1852.

## Design

The roll [Roll1851], read through the project's reader at its recorded
checksum, and the note's figure as [claim 002] states it (derived). Each
convention is a rule for which of the roll's rows count toward the
note's year, fixed in a protocol note before any count under it is run
(imposed), so that none is chosen for the number it gives:

- *ordinary days only*: the calendar year, the fairs left out, a count
  [claim 001] already gives as 49;
- *a year from 25 March*: 25 March 1851 to 24 March 1852, the older
  reckoning of the year, every row counted;
- *both*: that year, the fairs left out.

A convention gives the note's figure when its count is exactly
forty-eight.

## Tasks

1. **Fix the conventions** in a protocol note, each as a rule a program
   can apply, before any count under them is run; produces a dated note.
2. **Count the roll under each convention**, by a program that fails
   were a count otherwise, with a second route through the file's own
   lines; produces check 006 and claim 006.
3. **Bring what the counts show** to the researcher, with what it means
   for the cautions of [tension T001] and [tension T004].

## Sanity checks

Every check of plan 001 still holds, each program re-run and each written
procedure not invalidated by what changed. The round check is clean; the
indexes are in step.

## Deliverables

The protocol note; check 006 and claim 006; whatever change to the
tension rows the result calls for, at the researcher's direction; and,
before the first count on a year other than the calendar year, the
passages in `evidence_and_reasoning/terminology.md`,
`python_project/src/README.md`, and `python_project/src/roll.py` that
call the calendar year the frame of every count of the roll, qualified to
say so of every count that names no other.

## Confirmation and refutation

COMPLETE when each convention on the list has a count from a check that
would fail were the count otherwise, and a claim states which of them,
if any, gives forty-eight. That none does is a result like any other,
and leaves the note's convention open.

## Out of scope

Any convention off the list, which takes a plan of its own. Why the
note's author counted as they did. Whether the roll itself is right. The
count for 1852.
