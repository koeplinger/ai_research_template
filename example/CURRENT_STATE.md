# Current state of the research

*Created 6 September 2026; updated 6 September 2026.*

## Research goal

Whether a synthetic market roll supports the count of market days a
synthetic note states for 1851: the question as posed is
`evidence_and_reasoning/research_statement.md`, the thesis
`evidence_and_reasoning/problem_statement.md` (H-A: the two disagree).

## What is established

The roll holds 51 market days in 1851 [claim 001], and the note states
forty-eight [claim 002]; see `FINDINGS.md`. The monthly mean follows by
arithmetic [claim 003]. The 49 ordinary rows are Tuesdays and the two
fairs Thursdays, on 51 distinct dates, and three of the year's 52
Tuesdays have no row [claim 007]. The note, version 1, is released
(`paper/note_v1.md`).

## What is ruled out

Duplicated rows as the explanation of the roll's excess over the note
[claim 004].

## What is open

Why the two figures differ: a year beginning on 25 March [claim 005]
and a count of ordinary Tuesdays only [claim 006], both held by plan
002. The tension ledger's three rows, T001 (the count), T002 (the
weekday of the fairs), and T003 (the Tuesday in August without a row),
carry live cautions. The plans and their status:
`evidence_and_reasoning/research_plans/ROADMAP.md`.
